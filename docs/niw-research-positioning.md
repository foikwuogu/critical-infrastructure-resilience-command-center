# Research Portfolio Positioning

Project 4 integrates the portfolio into a coherent research direction:

1. Zero-Trust critical-infrastructure security
2. Post-quantum migration for legacy infrastructure
3. AI predictive maintenance
4. Cross-domain resilience command center

The strongest claim is not that the software is production-ready. The stronger research claim is that the work develops and evaluates a framework for integrating cyber, cryptographic and physical-resilience indicators into human-centered critical-infrastructure decision support.

Avoid claims of deployment at a real utility/operator unless independently documented.
